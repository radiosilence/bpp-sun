    How this works, and where it lies

    Shadows are ray-marched against the Environment Agency's 1m first-return LIDAR surface, so trees and buildings are the real scanned shapes. Bench positions, footprints and paths are from OpenStreetMap. A bench is sampled 1m above the ground every five minutes; the percentage is the share of daylight spent in direct sun.

    LIDAR is flown in winter, so crowns are filled in to approximate summer leaf. Tree shade is therefore pessimistic from November to March, when most of this canopy is bare. Rays may pass under a crown for up to 30m before trunks and undergrowth are assumed to block them. Cloud is not modelled. Benches missing from OpenStreetMap are missing here.
